01

Data controller

The controller of your personal data is FLASH of LIGHT, s. r. o., Kríková 4, 821 07 Bratislava, Company ID 48211222.

info@montaze-svietidiel.eu +421 902 842 055

02

Enquiries and personal data

The form prepares an email in your email application. The website itself does not send the form data to our server or store it. Processing begins only when you choose to send the prepared email to us.

The email may include your name, phone number, email address, installation location, selected service, project scope, preferred timing, message and any photos you choose to attach.

The purpose is to respond to your enquiry, prepare a technical solution or quotation and take steps before a possible contract. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — pre-contractual steps taken at your request. We do not ask for marketing consent for this purpose.

03

Retention period

We retain enquiry data only for as long as necessary to communicate with you and handle your request. If a contract is concluded, relevant data may be retained for the duration of the contractual relationship and afterwards as required by accounting, tax and record-keeping laws.

04

Recipients of the data

The data may be accessed only by authorised company personnel and necessary email or IT service providers. We do not use the data for automated decision-making or profiling. The technical processing of your email also depends on the provider you use to send it.

05

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR conditions, you may request access to your data, correction or deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. You can send your request to info@montaze-svietidiel.eu.

If you believe your data is not being handled lawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic ↗.

06

Cookies and external services

This website code does not use analytics, marketing or other optional cookies. The language choice is not stored in cookies or localStorage — the Slovak and English versions use separate addresses.

Fonts, photos and video are loaded directly from this website. Facebook, Google Maps and Daibau are simple outbound links; your browser connects to those services only after you click. We therefore do not currently display a cookie consent banner.

The hosting provider may process necessary server logs for security and technical purposes, such as the IP address, request time and requested file. These logs are not used for marketing tracking.
