Light fitting installation

LED technology

Upgrades and wiring designed to improve energy efficiency, brightness, performance and ease of control.

  • Installation and wiring of all types of LED strips
  • Conversion of existing light fittings to LED or other light sources
  • Upgrades to digitally dimmable and remote-controlled light sources

Servicing and electrical work

Repairs and additional electrical work for safe and reliable operation.

  • Repair and servicing of exclusive light fittings and electrical installations
  • Repair and installation of sockets, switches, junction boxes and circuit breakers
  • Battery replacement in emergency light fittings

03 — About us

Experience
you can see.

For more than 14 years, we have specialised in professional light fitting installation and servicing. We install and service all types of light fittings.

We have extensive experience installing light fittings in every category, from high-end and luxury pieces to affordable solutions, as well as a wide range of electrical systems including LED technology.

We can also advise you on choosing and purchasing chandeliers, light fittings, lamps, decorative lighting or LED strips. Our work with architects, private clients and businesses allows us to provide sound professional advice and consultations.
